# Recorded mechanical clock sounds

Both recordings are by **Joseph SARDIN**, BigSoundBank / LaSonothèque, and are released under **CC0 (public domain)**. Bundled locally for offline playback. Retrieved 18 October 2026.

- `clock-ticking.ogg`: [Tic Tac Mechanical Alarm Clock #5, sound 2816](https://bigsoundbank.com/tic-tac-mechanical-alarm-clock-5-s2816.html). Original Ogg download: https://bigsoundbank.com/UPLOAD/ogg/2816.ogg. A recording of a mechanical clock, described by the author as easy to loop.
- `clock-bell.mp3`: [Mechanical alarm clock, ringtone #8, sound 2811](https://bigsoundbank.com/mechanical-alarm-clock-ringtone-8-s2811.html). Original MP3 download: https://bigsoundbank.com/UPLOAD/mp3/2811.mp3. A recording of an old mechanical alarm clock ringing.

Files are unmodified. Playback levels are adjusted in the app. Two short recorded escapement strikes are extracted at runtime, gently faded, and placed one second apart to give a slower tick-tock without changing pitch. This loop plays only during a running timer. The bell plays at its original speed.
